# Third-party assets

The icons in `src/client/resources/assets/tierspoofer/textures/font/` aren't mine:

- `mctiers/`, `subtiers/`: from [mctiers-dev/TierTagger](https://github.com/mctiers-dev/TierTagger), MPL-2.0 (`licenses/TierTagger-LICENSE.txt`)
- `pvptiers/`: from [PvPTiers/Tiers](https://github.com/PvPTiers/Tiers), GPL-3.0 (`licenses/Tiers-GPL-3.0.txt`). The PvPTiers tier colors in `TierList.java` come from the same project.

Because of the PvPTiers icons, published builds of this mod are GPL-3.0.
